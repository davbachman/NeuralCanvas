Training learns the next-token probabilities. Most likely always chooses a token with the highest probability; Sample draws a token from the distribution. These are generation choices you can use with every generative model that follows.

Change the distribution without changing the model

In a copy, keep the original logits, loss and output Softmax. Add two independent probability branches at temperatures T = 0.5 and T = 2. Each must compute Softmax(logits / T) from the original vocabulary logits. Work out the Arithmetic expressions and wiring yourself. Save sampling.json.

The grader checks the two distributions and verifies that all learned parameters match base.json. This is the required submitted experiment.
