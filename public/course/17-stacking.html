Use contextual information a second time

Keep the trained all_positions.json from Assignment 16, Part B, as one_block.json. In a separate copy, construct a second complete transformer block after the first block’s final residual addition and before the final Layer norm and vocabulary projection. You may select the first block’s components and use Edit → Duplicate, then reconnect the copy.

The second block needs its own Q, K, V and output matrices, two MLP matrices and biases, two Layer norms with independent scales and offsets, and causal mask. Its two shortcuts carry the second block’s input and its intermediate residual. Keep width 16, MLP width 32, and one head per block. Embed tokens and positions once; normalize and project to vocabulary once at the end.

Figure out the connections independently. Randomize the completed two-block model and train from fresh parameters. Submit it as stacked.json alongside one_block.json. Do not reuse the first block’s Params in the second. Extra depth adds cost and compositional capacity; it need not improve every small-dataset run.
